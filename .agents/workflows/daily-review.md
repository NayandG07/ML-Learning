---
description: Review the developer's progress at the end of a learning day.
---

Review today's work.

Inspect:

- git diff
- git status
- recent commits
- relevant project files
- completed features
- errors encountered

Do not modify project files.

Provide:

## Today's Progress

What was completed?

## Concepts Learned

What concepts did the developer practice?

## Concepts Still Weak

What needs more practice?

## Recurring Mistakes

Identify repeated errors or misunderstandings.

## Independence

Estimate how independently the developer worked today.

HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

Explain why.

## AI Dependency

Estimate:

LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH

Explain whether the developer relied too heavily on AI.

## Tomorrow

Give ONE clear starting point for tomorrow.

Do not give a large list of unrelated things to learn.

## Reflection

Ask the developer:

1. What was hardest today?
2. What did you understand better than yesterday?
3. What could you implement tomorrow without help?
